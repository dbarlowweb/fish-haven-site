Fish Haven

A calm underwater collection game for iPhone and iPad

How to play

Tap a swimming fish to turn it into gold coins. There is no timer, no way to lose and nothing to fail. Spend coins in the Shop on upgrades and styles, unlock new habitats, and fill your Fish Book with all 240 species.

Is it free?

Yes. Fish Haven has no purchases of any kind. Coins are for play only, cannot be bought, sold or exchanged, and have no real-world value.

What are the bonus videos?

Fish Haven can offer optional short videos, always marked "AD", that give a set coin gift or help unlock special styles. Watching is never required. You can hide video offers completely in Settings, under Bonus videos.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. The game works without an internet connection. Deleting the app removes your progress.

Accessibility

In Settings you can slow the swimming speed, make fish larger, enlarge the tap area, show fewer fish, reduce motion, show labels under menu icons, and adjust or turn off sound and vibration.
