Privacy Policy

Fish Haven for iPhone and iPad. Last updated 25 November 2026.

The short version

Fish Haven does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or account details, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The only third party is Google AdMob, which provides the optional bonus videos.

Information stored on your device

Your coins, collection, upgrades and settings are saved in a file on your device so you can continue where you left off. This file is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Optional bonus videos (Google AdMob)

Fish Haven can show optional, clearly labelled videos provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Information about ads shown and how you interact with them
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the ads

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Tracking: Fish Haven asks once whether it may track. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Turn videos off: In Fish Haven, open Settings and switch off "Show video offers". No video offers are shown after that.
  • Personalised ads: You can also limit personalised ads in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising.

Children

Fish Haven is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Purchases

Fish Haven has no purchases. Coins are for play only and have no real-world value.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
